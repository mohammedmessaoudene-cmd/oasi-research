# Third-party notices

No third-party source tree or binary is vendored in this research preview.

The build and audit process uses external tools that are not redistributed as part of this repository, including Rust/Cargo, Python, Git, zlib and PDF tooling. Their versions are recorded in the SBOM, `tools/requirements-pdf.lock`, and reproducibility evidence; each remains under its own license. ReportLab, Pillow and charset-normalizer are build-only PDF dependencies.

QEMU, Linux, Buildroot, BusyBox, OpenSSL, elfutils, and other historical campaign dependencies are not included and are not relicensed by this repository. Historical references to them do not make them components of this source release.

Official license texts are included for the proposed licenses only and do not assert ownership of content that the project lacks authority to license.
