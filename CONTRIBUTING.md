# Contributing

Issues and private security reports are welcome after the repository becomes public. External code is not merged in `v0.1.0-research-preview` until a contribution policy protects provenance and any future licensing options.

For now:

- propose ideas, tests, reviews, and reproducibility reports;
- do not submit third-party code without clear provenance and license;
- preserve negative results and scope qualifiers;
- do not weaken fail-closed tests to obtain a pass;
- do not claim institutional endorsement.

A future contributor agreement or developer certificate process may be adopted. Nothing here transfers copyright or promises acceptance.
