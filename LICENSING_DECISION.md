# Strategic licensing decision for OASI v0.1

- Historical Rust source and tests: **`Apache-2.0 OR MIT`**.
- New original standalone project tools and schemas: **`Apache-2.0`**.
- Article, figures, specifications, documentation, and public evidence summaries: **`CC-BY-4.0`**.
- Third-party material: its original license only.
- OASI name and logo: project and trademark rights reserved; no registration claimed.

AGPL is not applied retroactively to v0.1. A future clean-slate OASI runtime may use `AGPL-3.0-only` only after a fresh chain-of-title, contributor, and compatibility review. No commercial dual license is claimed for v0.1.
