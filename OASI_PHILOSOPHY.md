# OASI philosophy

## Canonical name

**Operational Artificial System Intelligence (OASI)** is the canonical name of
this research paradigm. It denotes a system-level hypothesis in which operation,
artificial embodiment, memory, cognition, authority, and development are
coordinated through one versioned causal history and constitutionally mediated
effects.

Here, **operational** means pertaining to the operation of the system; it does
not assert production readiness. **System intelligence** is the research target,
not a claim of achieved general or superintelligence, consciousness, organismic
unity, deployment, external validation, or superiority. **Organismic computing**
names the architectural hypothesis developed below; it is not a competing
expansion of OASI.

## Canonical thesis

`OS = AI = continuous activity of one artificial organism.`

OASI is not an operating system with an assistant attached, and it is not an LLM placed in a privileged CPU ring. The research question is whether perception, action, memory, body constraints, repair, authority, and learning can participate in one developmental causal history.

The program separates four levels that must not be conflated:

1. **Vision** — the organismic thesis above.
2. **Constitutional foundations** — provenance, bounded authority, fail-closed effects, epochs, rollback, and quarantine.
3. **Implemented reference mechanisms** — the bounded user-space AERA runtime in this repository.
4. **Unproven capabilities** — complete organogenesis, a learned general body schema, long-horizon embodied cognition, hardware integration, and general superiority.

Hashes, manifests, leases, and receipts are constitutional or immune-system mechanisms. They are not by themselves the complete organism.

## Asymmetric development

The working architectural intuition is cognition-to-reflex distillation: expensive deliberation may, after repeated safe experience, produce a narrowly scoped reflex. A reflex remains subordinate to body/epoch authority, expiration, provenance, and commit-time revalidation. The T4 implementation showed that a poor or frozen reflex can cause harm; therefore reflex formation is a falsifiable mechanism, not a slogan.

## Current epistemic status

The broad OASI thesis is neither demonstrated nor refuted. T4 refuted narrower claims in its frozen domain and failed to operationalize broad non-separability. AERA is an engineering contribution in a bounded user-space setting, not a proof of the whole philosophy.
