# v0.2 provenance and canonical naming addendum

The existing owner rights and provenance declaration remains applicable to the
repository. For v0.2, the owner additionally authorized publication of the
sanitized S5/S6 local fixture code, data, reports, and revised preprint. No
other human author or contributor is asserted for these added files.

The public tree excludes operational authorization text, private paths,
recovery artifacts, secrets, conversations, and frozen internal generations.
The S5/S6 code is original project code; `cryptography` and SQLite are external
runtime dependencies and are not vendored. The embedded RFC 8032 key material
is a public test fixture, not a secret.

For this version, the author records **Operational Artificial System
Intelligence (OASI)** as the canonical name of the research paradigm. The
descriptive article subtitle---*An Organismic Computing Architecture for
Body-Bound Runtime Assurance and Developmental OS-AI Integration*---identifies
the current architectural hypothesis and assurance focus; it is not an
alternative expansion of OASI. `Operational` refers to system operation, not
production maturity or deployment status.

The versioned commit, deterministic archive, article, and reserved DOI records
are intended to form a public, hash-addressed provenance trail when published.
They document the author's terminology and release lineage; they do not by
themselves decide legal novelty, priority, patentability, trademark ownership,
or exclusive rights.

This addendum records a bounded provenance representation. It is not legal
advice, an institutional rights decision, a patentability determination, or an
external scientific validation.
