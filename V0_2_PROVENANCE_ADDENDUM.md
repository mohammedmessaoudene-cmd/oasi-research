# v0.2 provenance addendum

The existing owner rights and provenance declaration remains applicable to the
repository. For v0.2, the owner additionally authorized publication of the
sanitized S5/S6 local fixture code, data, reports, and revised preprint. No
other human author or contributor is asserted for these added files.

recovery artifacts, secrets, conversations, and frozen internal generations.

runtime dependencies and are not vendored. The embedded RFC 8032 key material
is a public test fixture, not a secret.

This addendum records a bounded provenance representation. It is not legal
advice, an institutional rights decision, a patentability determination, or an
external scientific validation.
