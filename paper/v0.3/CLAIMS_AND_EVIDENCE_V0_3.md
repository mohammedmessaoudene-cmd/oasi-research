## Promotion rule

A claim may be strengthened only when the same immutable evidence object supplies:

1. exact artifact identity and manifest;
2. the relevant preregistered gate;
3. raw command, stdout, stderr, and return code;
4. an independently executable verifier;
5. fresh cross-review and red-team results;
6. an explicit claim delta and non-claims.

Narrative progress messages, screenshots, or aggregate gate counts are insufficient.
