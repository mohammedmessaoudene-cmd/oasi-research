# Hostile Reviewer 2 Report — OASI v0.3

## Recommendation

**Major-revision threshold passed for an architecture/preprint paper; empirical validation remains required before a systems-performance claim.**

## Principal objections examined

### 1. “Organismic” may be a metaphor without a mechanism
The revised manuscript now defines operational monism through one versioned causal history, organ projections, a constitutional transition, and a hidden-authority exclusion. The term is therefore connected to testable architecture rather than used as a decorative analogy.

**Residual concern:** longitudinal developmental closure is not yet implemented.

### 2. The contribution may reduce to a monolith with capabilities
The finite-horizon equivalence proposition and the mandatory generic-monolith baseline directly acknowledge this possibility. The manuscript no longer claims that shared memory, a single process, or fewer messages are organismic value.

**Residual concern:** a positive residual value has not been demonstrated.

### 3. AERA resembles runtime assurance and capability checks
The positioning against Simplex and classical protection is now explicit. The defensible difference is the body/epoch/generation/evidence binding of learned competence at commit, not runtime assurance in the abstract.

**Residual concern:** no independently developed implementation exists.

### 4. Formalism may exceed implementation
Proposed developmental equations are labeled as research targets. Implemented evidence is separated from specification and engineering substrate. The AERA proposition is conditional on snapshot identity.

**Residual concern:** the trusted computing base remains broad.

### 5. T4 is negative and construct-limited
The paper preserves that result and explains what it refutes: shared state alone. It does not use the construct-validity defect to erase the negative outcome.

### 6. Internal review may create common-mode bias
The manuscript names this threat. Contradictory agents and clean-room verifiers reduce, but do not remove, common-mode assumptions.

### 7. Biology may imply consciousness or feelings
The revised text defines homeostasis, body, and organ in narrow engineering terms and excludes biological equivalence and consciousness.

### 8. The paper could be overloaded by engineering incidents
The incident history is summarized only to establish the evidence discipline; generation numbers and operational details remain outside the main scientific narrative.

## Final view

The manuscript is now a rigorous architecture and research-program paper, not a claim that an OASI organism has been completed. Its publication value rests on the exact formalization of the philosophy, AERA, the preserved negative result, and the unusually explicit promotion boundary. The decisive next contribution must be external and empirical.
