# Roadmap

## Near term

- obtain explicit ownership, affiliation, funding, and patent-impact decisions;
- publish the bounded `v0.1.0-research-preview` only after digest-bound human approval;
- obtain independent external reproduction of the public test suite;
- stabilize AERA terminology and a versioned conformance schema;
- add reproducible Linux CI without weakening current tests.

## Research

- define an identifiable mechanism for developmental body-schema learning;
- design a causally independent modular comparator with messages upstream of action;
- preregister a new experiment only after the Mechanism Gate passes;
- measure reflex promotion, invalidation, regret, damage, and readaptation under distribution shift;
- explore kernel, VM, and hardware integration only as separate, explicitly authorized tracks.

No roadmap item is a promised capability or evidence of current readiness.
