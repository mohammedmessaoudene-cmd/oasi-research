# v0.3.0-research-preview — engineering update, not a superiority claim

## Added
An experimental local HASH_COUNT_LOCAL service, persistent per-workspace cumulative admission cap, explicit CLI workflow and an archive-only portable budget/result oracle. The CLI source is the frozen September 6 candidate, not a post-hoc repair of D1. D1 remains noncompliant with its own exhausted budget; this separate lot does not retroactively change it.

## Evidence and scope
One historical campaign: 16 reservations, 16 claims/start markers, 15 correct unique results and one controlled interrupted execution followed by separately budgeted resume. 168 snapshots; 70 command receipts (62 success exits, 6 refusal exits, 2 controlled-exit receipts). These two controlled exits include a pre-worker exit; they are not two started interrupted jobs. 28 decisions/feedback events are counted, not a demonstration of intelligence.

The portable verifier rechecks the original archive through an explicit opaque-path-to-member map, and the sanitized derivative, without importing the service. It validates integrity, chain links, cumulative histories, reservation bindings, result uniqueness and exact fixture outputs. It does not revalidate complete AERA decisions, learning mathematics, OS/process identity, all schedules or a power-loss model.

## Remaining limitations
No separate standalone SHA256 campaign. Per-workspace cooperative cap, not protection against privileged rollback. No full service installation/runtime portability qualification. No native binary in this release; rebuilding with the supplied historical Windows script is an explicit new action, not done here. No Linux PID1 isolation claim for this host service. Controlled interruptions are not power cuts. AI-assisted review is not external peer review. No comparative scientific advantage or complete OASI claim.
